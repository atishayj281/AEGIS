# Employee Handbook - Public Policies

**Effective Date:** 01-Jan-2026  
**Classification:** Public - All Employees

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM local time.

## Data Security Policy

- Never share passwords or API keys via email or chat
- Use company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems remotely
- Report suspected security incidents within 1 hour to security@company.com
- Lock workstations when leaving desk

## Code of Conduct

All employees must maintain professional behavior, respect confidentiality of internal documentation, and follow applicable laws and regulations.

## Expense Reimbursement

Submit expense reports within 30 days. Receipts required for expenses over $25.

## Leave Policy

- Annual leave: 20 days per year
- Sick leave: 10 days per year
- Parental leave: per local regulations
