# Compliance Audit Report 2026

**Audit Date:** 15-Jan-2026  
**Auditor:** Internal Compliance Team  
**Classification:** Confidential - Compliance Officers and Admin Only

## Executive Summary

The annual compliance audit was completed on 15-Jan-2026. The organization demonstrates strong overall compliance posture with three medium-risk findings requiring remediation within 90 days.

## Key Findings

1. **Medium Risk:** Encryption policy requires updates to align with AES-256 standards for data at rest.
2. **Medium Risk:** Customer data retention procedures need documentation updates for GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure).
3. **Medium Risk:** Access review process for privileged accounts should be conducted quarterly instead of annually.

## Positive Observations

- No critical violations detected
- All financial reporting controls operating effectively
- Audit trail logging is comprehensive and tamper-evident
- Employee security training completion rate: 98%

## Recommendations

- Update encryption policy by 15-Apr-2026
- Implement automated data retention workflows
- Schedule quarterly privileged access reviews

## Customer Data Retention Requirements

Under GDPR and internal policy:
- Customer PII must be retained for 7 years for financial records
- Marketing consent data may be retained for 3 years
- Right to erasure requests must be processed within 30 days
- Data minimization principles must be applied at collection
