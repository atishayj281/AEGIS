# GDPR Data Retention Policy

**Document ID:** POL-COMP-001  
**Classification:** Compliance Records

## Purpose

This policy defines retention periods and procedures for customer personal data in compliance with GDPR and applicable regulations.

## Retention Periods

| Data Category | Retention Period | Legal Basis |
|---------------|------------------|-------------|
| Customer account data | 7 years after account closure | Legal obligation |
| Transaction records | 7 years | Financial regulations |
| Marketing preferences | 3 years or until consent withdrawn | Legitimate interest |
| Support tickets | 2 years | Legitimate interest |
| Website analytics (anonymized) | 26 months | Legitimate interest |

## Right to Erasure

Customers may request deletion of personal data. Requests must be:
- Verified within 5 business days
- Processed within 30 calendar days
- Documented in the compliance tracking system

## Data Minimization

Collect only data necessary for the stated purpose. Review data collection forms annually.

## Breach Notification

Report personal data breaches to the DPO within 24 hours. Notify supervisory authority within 72 hours if required.
